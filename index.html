The Purpose of 60FPS

Mapping the real-time health of consequential industries, digital infrastructure, and market structures that shape our economic reality.

Tracking the Modern Economic Tollbooth

The modern economy functions less like traditional industrial production and increasingly like an ad-driven digital ecosystem where recommendation engines and platforms act as the ultimate middleman. 60FPS tracks these underlying market structures—from the Magnificent 10 technology giants to sovereign debt curves—to expose where capital truly flows, how value is extracted, and how corporate balance sheets decouple from real human output.

Empowering Consumers and Restoring Market Agency

As retail spend migrates into walled digital gardens and brand loyalty is cannibalized by platform lock-in, consumers face an asymmetrical information landscape. 60FPS provides transparent, institutional-grade telemetry directly to market participants. By understanding how algorithmic distribution, capital financialization, and corporate stock-promotion work behind the scenes, consumers and independent investors can reclaim agency over their economic footprint.

Resisting Financial Manipulation for Long-Term Value

True economic health cannot be measured by raw GDP volume or hollow equity valuation inflation alone. 60FPS is built to anchor financial tracking in resilient fundamentals, corporate transparency, and sustainable capital allocation—balancing the reality of the profit motive with the necessity for genuine societal well-being.
